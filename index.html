Interactive 3D Storytelling for Modern Luxury Brands

Static display ads are easy to scroll past. Interactive rich media pairs lightweight 3D product exploration with motion-led storytelling—turning passive impressions into tactile brand moments.

Instead of asking shoppers to imagine how light catches a diamond or how a silhouette moves, real-time WebGL lets them rotate the piece 360 degrees, inspect fine details, and engage directly inside the browser.

From Passive Impressions to Active Exploration

Rich media transforms an ordinary placement into an inviting digital showroom. Instead of telling a shopper that a piece of jewellery is intricate, you let them rotate it 360°, zoom into the diamond setting, and see how light catches every facet directly in the browser.

“The best rich media doesn’t interrupt the page—it earns attention by feeling effortless, tactile, and tailored to the product.”

Designed for Speed, Clarity, and Engagement

Using hardware-accelerated GSAP pinning and WebGL rendering, brands achieve higher dwell time, stronger recall, and deeper product consideration without heavy page reloads.
